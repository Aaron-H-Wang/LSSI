\section{Conclusion}

This paper proposed a Sparse Identification of Nonlinear Functions (SINF) framework that discovers explicit magnetic core loss equations from measured data. SINF casts core loss modeling as a data-driven symbolic regression problem without presuming a functional form, and constructs a comprehensively expanded library of symbolic candidate terms. The dominant candidates are isolated from the library by a data-driven sparse regression and their coefficients are optimized maximize accuracy. Experiments discover an explicit equation with only $7$ active terms out of $103$ candidates, achieving a MAPE of $1.56\%$ and an $R^2$ of $0.9997$, providing a a feasible and accurate solution for magnetic characterization and design.
